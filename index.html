常見問題

怎麼啟用鍵盤？
iPhone「設定」→ 一般 → 鍵盤 → 鍵盤 → 新增鍵盤 → 選「快打鍵盤」。回到鍵盤清單點進「快打鍵盤」，開啟「允許完整取用」。之後在任何輸入框長按 🌐 切換。

為什麼需要「允許完整取用」？
讓 App 與鍵盤共用快速詞與版面、記錄剪貼歷史（可關閉）、按鍵震動。App 沒有任何網路功能，不會上傳資料。不開啟時仍可打英文與使用計算機。

鍵盤上看不到我的快速詞？
請確認已開啟「允許完整取用」，然後收起鍵盤再叫出一次。

剪貼歷史沒有紀錄？
請確認已開啟「允許完整取用」，且 App 內「設定 → 歷史 → 記錄剪貼歷史」是開啟的。鍵盤打開時才會自動記錄；不開鍵盤要記錄，可照 App 內「設定 → 歷史 → 不開鍵盤也能記錄」設定捷徑。密碼管理員標記為機密的內容不會記錄。

可以打英文或注音嗎？
英文可以：切到鍵盤上方的「🔤 英文」分頁，打字時會出現單字建議，點一下才會替換。注音目前沒有，請長按 🌐 切回系統鍵盤。

密碼欄位為什麼不能用？
iOS 規定密碼等安全欄位一律使用系統鍵盤。

怎麼刪除資料？
可在 App 內刪除詞語、清除計算歷史與剪貼歷史。刪除 App 會一併刪除資料。

FAQ

How do I enable the keyboard?
iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → choose “Quick Input”. Back in the keyboard list, tap “Quick Input” and turn on “Allow Full Access”. Then press and hold 🌐 in any text field to switch.

Why is “Allow Full Access” needed?
To share your phrases and layout between the app and the keyboard, to record clipboard history (optional), and for key haptics. The app has no networking features and uploads nothing. English typing and the calculator work without it.

My phrases do not appear on the keyboard.
Check that “Allow Full Access” is on, then dismiss the keyboard and open it again.

Clipboard history is empty.
Check that “Allow Full Access” is on and that Settings → History → Record clipboard history is enabled in the app. Text is recorded automatically only while the keyboard is open; to save without opening it, set up a shortcut as shown in the app under Settings → History. Items marked confidential by password managers are skipped.

Can I type letters with it?
Yes, in English: open the “🔤 English” tab above the keys. Word suggestions appear as you type and replace text only when you tap them. For other languages, press and hold 🌐 to switch keyboards.

Why is it unavailable in password fields?
iOS always uses the system keyboard in secure fields.

How do I delete my data?
Delete phrases and clear calculation and clipboard history inside the app. Deleting the app removes its data.
