快打鍵盤 隱私政策

生效日期：2026-11-26
開發者：Guang Jie Lee
聯絡信箱：gjleecc@gmail.com

一句話版本：你的詞語、計算內容和剪貼歷史只存在你的手機裡，不會傳給開發者或任何第三方。

一、我們不蒐集你的資料

「快打鍵盤」不需要帳號，不含任何網路連線功能，不使用分析工具、廣告或第三方 SDK。你輸入的文字、詞語、計算內容，都不會被傳送到開發者或任何第三方。

二、資料存放在哪裡

以下資料只存放在你的手機（App 與鍵盤共用的本機資料夾）：

  • 快速詞（內容、分類、置頂、使用次數與最近使用時間）
  • 計算機版面、自訂快捷鍵（含你自己選的圖示）
  • 鍵盤設定
  • 計算歷史（最近 30 筆）
  • 剪貼歷史（預設開啟，可在設定關閉；最多 20 筆，每筆最多 2000 字）

你可以在 App 內刪除詞語、清除計算歷史與剪貼歷史。刪除 App 會一併刪除這些資料。這些資料可能包含在你自己的 iPhone 備份中，由 Apple 依你的設定處理。

三、關於「允許完整取用」

鍵盤的「允許完整取用」用於：

  • 讓 App 與鍵盤共用你的快速詞與版面
  • （選用）讀取剪貼簿以記錄剪貼歷史
  • 按鍵震動

我們不會用它來記錄你的按鍵，也不會把任何內容傳出手機。不開啟完整取用時，鍵盤仍可使用計算機，但可能無法讀到 App 內的快速詞。

四、鍵盤如何處理你輸入的文字

鍵盤不會儲存你輸入框裡的文字。計算機的「輸入」模式與清除、復原功能，只在記憶體中暫時讀取游標附近的文字，離開鍵盤後即清除。密碼等安全輸入欄位，iOS 本來就不會使用第三方鍵盤。

五、語音輸入

鍵盤下方的麥克風是 iPhone 系統的聽寫功能，由 Apple 依其隱私政策處理，本 App 不接觸你的語音內容。

六、兒童

本 App 不針對兒童，也不蒐集任何人的個人資料。

七、政策變更

若有變更，會在 App 更新時說明，並更新生效日期。

Quick Input Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-26
Developer: Guang Jie Lee
Contact: gjleecc@gmail.com

In short: your phrases, calculations and clipboard history stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer or any third party.

1. We do not collect your data

Quick Input needs no account, has no networking features, and uses no analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. The text, phrases and calculations you enter are never sent anywhere.

2. Where your data is stored

The following data is stored only on your phone (in the local folder shared by the app and the keyboard):

  • Quick phrases (text, category, pin, use count and last-used time)
  • Calculator layout and custom shortcut keys (including icons you pick)
  • Keyboard settings
  • Calculation history (latest 30 entries)
  • Clipboard history (on by default and can be turned off in Settings; up to 20 entries, up to 2000 characters each)

You can delete phrases and clear calculation and clipboard history inside the app. Deleting the app deletes this data. It may be included in your own iPhone backups, which Apple handles according to your settings.

3. About "Allow Full Access"

"Allow Full Access" is used to:

  • let the app and keyboard share your phrases and layout
  • (optional) read the clipboard to record clipboard history
  • provide key haptics

We never use it to record your keystrokes, and nothing leaves your phone. Without Full Access the keyboard still offers the calculator, but cannot sync with the app.

4. How the keyboard handles what you type

The keyboard does not store the text in your input fields. The calculator's Type mode and the clear and undo features only read text near the cursor temporarily in memory, cleared when you leave the keyboard. iOS never uses third-party keyboards in secure fields such as passwords.

5. Voice input

The microphone below the keyboard is the iPhone's built-in dictation, handled by Apple under its privacy policy. This app never accesses your voice.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children and does not collect anyone's personal data.

7. Changes to this policy

Any change will be described in an app update, and the effective date will be updated.
